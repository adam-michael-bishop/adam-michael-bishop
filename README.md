## About Me
I'm a Full Stack Software Developer with experience in **web development, debugging, and AI model training**. Skilled in object-oriented programming, I enjoy maintaining and improving complex codebases, writing robust tests, and tackling challenging technical problems.  

Outside of work, I love **game development** — building projects with **Unity, Unreal Engine, and most recently Godot** in my spare time. I've always been a super passionate gamer growing up, and now I really enjoy learning about game development and creating my own games!

My background also includes **network engineering** and hands-on experience in both **software development** and **AI data annotation**.
